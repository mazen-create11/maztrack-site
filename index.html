How a brief is built

Four steps, each one inspectable. The model never sees anything that is not in a public record, and it is instructed to say when a fact is missing rather than fill it in.

01 · Registry

Identity and structure

SIREN, NAF activity, creation date, INSEE headcount band, employer status, open establishments, executive on record, latest filed accounts, labels (Qualiopi, RGE, ESS).

api.gouv.fr ↗
02 · BODACC

Legal notices

Sales and transfers (a new decision-maker), recent modifications, filed accounts, and collective procedures, which exclude the company from any outreach.

bodacc.fr open data ↗
03 · Score

Deterministic rules

0 to 100, same weights as the local engine: employer +18, under 24 months +15, recent sale +12, modification +6, several sites +6. Qualiopi caps at 25. Each point is listed with its reason.

scoring.ts ↗
04 · Claude

Brief and email

Claude Opus 5.5 receives the facts and the score, answers in a fixed JSON schema: summary, angle, readings, email, cautions. Prompt cached, effort low, about 20 seconds.

The engine behind the demo

Radar is the public face of a local prospecting engine in use since June 2026. The engine does what the demo cannot: it sources whole segments, keeps the data on the user's machine, and tracks what each lead becomes.

  • Sourcing by segment. NAF codes × departments, active companies only, paged through the registry at the official rate limit.
  • Scoring with hiring signals. Same rules as Radar, plus France Travail job postings (+25 to +35 when a company is recruiting now).
  • GDPR exclusions by default. Non-disclosable records, sole traders, companies over 250 staff and anything under collective procedure never enter the pipeline.
  • Local-first. Node 22+, SQLite built into Node, no external database, no telemetry. Runs as a macOS app or from the terminal.
  • Deal ledger. Timestamped proof of who introduced which account, commission tiers, reconciliation when a partner under-reports.
  • Exports. CSV with injection protection, XLSX written without dependencies.

Company

Maztrack is built in Aix-en-Provence by Mazen Chaban, a solo founder working as a registered sole proprietor since June 2026. It started as the tool behind an introducer business: finding small companies likely to buy training and consulting, then handing them to a partner firm. No outside funding. Costs today are the Anthropic API and two domains. The next step is a hosted version for small sales teams in France, where public registries are rich and under-used.
