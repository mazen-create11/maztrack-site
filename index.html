The pipeline, step by step

Each step is inspectable. The model never sees anything that is not in a public record, and it is told to say when a fact is missing rather than fill it in.

  1. 1

    Registry

    api.gouv.fr ↗

    SIREN, NAF activity, creation date, INSEE headcount band, employer status, open establishments, officer on record, latest filed accounts, labels (Qualiopi, RGE, ESS). One request, no key, official data.

  2. 2

    BODACC

    bodacc.fr open data ↗

    Sales and transfers (a new decision-maker), recent modifications, filed accounts, and collective procedures, which exclude the company from any outreach at all.

  3. 3

    Score

    scoring.ts ↗

    0 to 100 with the engine's default weights: employer +18, under 24 months +15, recent sale +12, modification +6, several sites +6. Qualiopi caps at 25. Every point is listed with its reason, nothing is hidden in a model.

  4. 4

    Claude

    brief.ts ↗

    Claude Opus 5.5 receives the facts and the score and answers in a fixed JSON schema: summary, angle, readings, email, cautions. System prompt cached, effort low, about 20 seconds. A refusal or a parse failure returns the facts alone, never a made-up brief.

The engine behind the demo

Radar is the public face of a local prospecting engine in use since June 2026. The engine does what the demo cannot: it sources whole segments, keeps the data on the user's machine, and tracks what each lead becomes.

  • Sourcing by segment. NAF codes × departments, active companies only, paged through the registry at the official rate limit.
  • Scoring with hiring signals. Same rules as Radar, plus France Travail job postings: +25 to +35 when a company is recruiting now.
  • GDPR exclusions by default. Non-disclosable records, sole traders, companies over 250 staff and anything under collective procedure never enter the pipeline.
  • Local-first. Node 22+, SQLite built into Node, no external database, no telemetry. Runs as a macOS app or from the terminal.
  • Deal ledger. Timestamped proof of who introduced which account, commission tiers, reconciliation when a partner under-reports.
  • Exports. CSV with injection protection, XLSX written without dependencies.
